Ink Point

Dodge the ink. A roguelike survival game where you play a cursor, enemies materialise out of the page, and your only weapons are the power-ups you pick up along the way.

How to play

  • Move with the mouse, with WASD / ZQSD / arrow keys, or with the on-screen joystick on a phone.
  • Power-ups fire the instant you touch them — there is nothing to aim.
  • Survive each wave, pick an upgrade, and climb the leaderboard.
  • Free, no account, no install. Available in English and French.
